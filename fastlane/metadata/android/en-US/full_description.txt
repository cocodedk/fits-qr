FITS shows three FITS contact cards, each with a QR code. A QR code is a square picture that a phone camera can read. Point a compatible camera or QR scanner at a code, and it offers to save that person as a contact.

The cards belong to Bassil Salameh (CEO), Babak Bandpey (CTO) and Silas Stilling Jørgensen (Cybersecurity Developer). Swipe left or right to move between them. After the last card you come back to the first. The FITS logo and tagline stay at the top.

Each QR code holds the person's name, role, organisation, work phone, work email, the FITS office address in Roskilde and https://fits.dk, in a standard contact format (vCard 3.0). Whoever scans a code gets only that one card.

Private and offline: the app makes the QR codes on your phone with ZXing, a code library, so it needs no internet access and shows no permission prompts. The small "i" button in the top corner (named "About this app") opens a screen with the version, the privacy policy and links to the website and the source code. These links open in your browser, and only when you tap them.
